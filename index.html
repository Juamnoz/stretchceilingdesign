The Material

What Is a Stretch Ceiling?

A stretch ceiling is a PVC or polyester membrane tensioned across a lightweight perimeter frame — creating a perfectly smooth, seamless surface with no visible joints. Fast to install, easy to maintain, resistant to moisture and mold, and fully customizable in color, texture, print, and integrated lighting.

  • Custom printed finishes
  • Integrated LED lighting
  • Any space, any style
